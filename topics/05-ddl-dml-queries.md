# DDL & DML Queries

## Key Points to Cover
- DDL: `CREATE`, `ALTER`, `DROP`, `TRUNCATE` for databases, schemas, tables, warehouses
- DML: `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE`, `MERGE`
- Transactions in Snowflake (`BEGIN`, `COMMIT`, `ROLLBACK`)
- Table types: permanent, transient, temporary
- Constraints (not enforced by default, except NOT NULL)
